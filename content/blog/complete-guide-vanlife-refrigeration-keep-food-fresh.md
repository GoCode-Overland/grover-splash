<p><strong>Why our community loves them:</strong></p>

<li><strong>Works in any weather</strong> – Our community uses them from Death Valley to Alaska</li>

### ❌ Thermoelectric Coolers (Skip These)

<p>We hate to be the bearers of bad news, but we've seen too many vanlifers learn this lesson the hard way:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Power hungry</strong> – Can use 2-3x more energy than compressor units</li>
<li><strong>Temperature dependent</strong> – Only cools 40°F below ambient (useless in hot weather)</li>
<li><strong>Inconsistent performance</strong> – We've seen food spoil because of temperature fluctuations</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Community wisdom:</strong> As Jake from our Arizona crew puts it, "I spent more on replacing spoiled food in two months than I would've spent upgrading to a proper compressor fridge." Now he uses Grover's 🏕️ campground finder to locate spots with reliable power hookups when needed.</p>
