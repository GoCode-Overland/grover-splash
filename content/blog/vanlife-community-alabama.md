<section id="destinations">
<h2>Top Vanlife Destinations in Alabama</h2>

Alabama offers incredible diversity for vanlifers, from mountain adventures to beach relaxation. Here are the must-visit destinations that every vanlifer should experience:

<div class="destination-card">
<h4>Oak Mountain State Park</h4>
<p>Alabama's largest state park offers excellent vanlife amenities with hookups, dump stations, and hiking trails. The park is particularly popular during fall foliage season and offers easy access to Birmingham for supplies and community events.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Hiking, mountain biking, and connecting with local vanlife community</p>
</div>

<div class="destination-card">
<h4>Gulf Shores State Park</h4>
<p>Alabama's crown jewel for beach-loving vanlifers. The park offers both RV sites and primitive camping, with direct beach access and excellent fishing opportunities. The nearby town of Gulf Shores is very van-friendly with numerous resources.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Beach camping, fishing, and warm winter escapes</p>
</div>

<div class="destination-card">
<h4>DeSoto State Park</h4>
<p>Located in the Appalachian foothills, this park offers some of Alabama's most scenic vanlife camping. The park features waterfalls, hiking trails, and cooler temperatures during summer months.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Mountain scenery, hiking, and summer cool-downs</p>
</div>

<div class="destination-card">
<h4>Cheaha State Park</h4>
<p>Home to Alabama's highest point, this park offers spectacular views and excellent van camping facilities. The park is particularly popular with vanlifers seeking mountain adventures without leaving Alabama.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Mountain views, hiking, and scenic drives</p>

<section id="seasonal-tips">
<h2>Seasonal Vanlife Tips for Alabama</h2>

Alabama's climate presents unique opportunities and challenges for vanlifers throughout the year. Here's what you need to know:

<h3>Spring (March-May)</h3>
<p>Spring is arguably the best time for vanlife in Alabama. Temperatures are mild, humidity is lower, and the state's natural beauty is at its peak with blooming flowers and comfortable camping weather.</p>

<div class="highlight-box">
<h4>Spring Vanlife Tips:</h4>
<ul>
<li>Book popular state parks early - this is peak season</li>
<li>Pack for temperature swings - Alabama springs can be unpredictable</li>
<li>Take advantage of numerous vanlife events and meetups</li>
<li>Explore the state's many hiking trails before summer heat</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3>Summer (June-August)</h3>
<p>Alabama summers are hot and humid, but with proper preparation, vanlifers can still enjoy the state's offerings. The Gulf Coast becomes particularly attractive with sea breezes and beach access.</p>

<ul>
<li>Focus on coastal areas for natural cooling</li>
<li>Invest in good ventilation and cooling systems</li>
<li>Plan activities for early morning and evening</li>
<li>Take advantage of state park facilities with air conditioning</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fall (September-November)</h3>
<p>Fall rivals spring as the ideal time for Alabama vanlife. Temperatures drop, humidity decreases, and the state's foliage puts on a spectacular show, especially in the northern regions.</p>

<h3>Winter (December-February)</h3>
<p>Alabama's mild winters make it an excellent escape for vanlifers fleeing harsh northern climates. The Gulf Coast remains comfortable, and indoor attractions become more appealing.</p>
